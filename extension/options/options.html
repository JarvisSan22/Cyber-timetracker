Dictionaries

Used by the side bar to look up the word you click. Build the files once on this machine with python3 scripts/fetch-dicts.py, reload the extension, then import here (takes a minute; stays in this browser).

Known words

Words from your Anki decks are coloured in the subtitle bar: accent colour = not in your decks, dotted underline = in Anki but not known yet, plain = known (card interval at least the days below). The side panel shows the picked word's status and warns when it is already mined. The Cyber Miner decks (Word field) always count; add your other vocabulary decks with the field that holds the word. Needs the dictionaries above.

Colours in the subtitle bar

With a new deck most words are not in Anki yet: "No colour" there and a colour for Known shows what you already know instead.

Cards

Netflix pictures

Netflix's video is copy-protected, so its picture comes from a screenshot of the tab. Chrome allows that only with access to all sites, or for a short while after the extension's hotkey or toolbar icon was used in the tab. Granting access here makes Netflix pictures work every time. Cyber Miner still runs only on YouTube and Netflix; you can remove the access again here or in Chrome's extension settings.

Condensed audio

The side panel's Transcript tab records every subtitle line of the video into one MP3 and leaves out the pauses between lines. It plays the lines in real time, so it takes about as long as the MP3 will be.
